Recommendation: Concept A — Critter Crew

It is the only direction that works for a 3-year-old and can mature for a 12-year-old without changing identity: the shape kit and palette stay, while scene complexity, text density and reward style scale with age. Its six subject buddies give every future game a built-in guide, and thick outlines plus flat fills keep numerals and objects readable on small phones. Flat vector parts map one-to-one onto SVG today and Rive bones later. Paper Garden is lovely but low-contrast and textures cost performance and animation effort; Star Arcade suits teens but dark neon with pulsing glow is harder on accessibility (contrast, photosensitivity) and feels intense for preschoolers. Implementation uses Concept A only.
