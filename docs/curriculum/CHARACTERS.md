# New characters per library (proposal)

Owner: curriculum-designer (proposal) → **creative-director decides** (D-004, CHARACTER_SYSTEM.md "Rules for new characters").
Status: concepts only. No art exists yet. Names and designs need a trademark and likeness check before adoption.

## 1. Style rules every new character follows (from `docs/CHARACTER_SYSTEM.md`)
- **Round and chunky:** pear, bean, blob or egg body; no sharp corners or skinny limbs.
- **Big friendly eyes** high and wide with a white shine; pink cheeks.
- **One signature thing** readable at 48 px (silhouette test).
- 5–5.5 px ink outline `#2B2140` at a 200-unit viewBox; soft white highlight on the upper left.
- The 7 states: idle, greeting, talking, encouraging, thinking, celebrating, retry, plus a reduced-motion pose set. Retry never shakes or looks disappointed in the child.
- Voice identity brief before any recording; no imitation of identifiable performers or existing characters.
- No resemblance to existing commercial characters (creative-director sign-off).

## 3. New hosts

### Loopi (computer science)
- **Type / silhouette:** monster caterpillar made of 4 round, stackable segments (each segment a soft rounded block), one antenna with a light bulb tip. Segments can show a tiny arrow icon (command blocks).
- **Colour:** lime `#A8D83F` with a darker lime belly; antenna light uses sparkle yellow.
- **Personality:** literal-minded, cheerful and methodical; does exactly what the cards say, and finds bugs funny rather than frustrating.
- **Voice direction:** bright, crisp, slightly bouncy rhythm; says "beep" only as a happy tic, not as robot speech.
- **State notes:** thinking = antenna light blinks slowly; celebrating = segments ripple like a wave; retry = segments sit down in a neat row (no droop of the face).
- **Similarity check:** caterpillar characters are common in children's media; keep the segment-as-block idea, avoid a hookah, a top hat or very hungry-caterpillar colour patterns.

### Zephy (pilot ground school)
- **Type / silhouette:** round albatross chick with oversized, stubby wings, aviator goggles pushed up on her forehead and a long red scarf that streams behind.
- **Colour:** cream body `#FFF1D0` (needs the ink outline for contrast), signal-red scarf `#E5484D`, grey wing tips.
- **Personality:** brave dreamer and careful planner who loves checklists ("check it twice, fly it once" is her bio motto, not a gameplay line).
- **Voice direction:** warm, steady, confident; calm in tricky moments like a good instructor.
- **State notes:** greeting = salute-wave with a wing; thinking = taps goggles; celebrating = little hop with scarf flutter (reduced motion: scarf still).
- **Similarity check:** avoid any airline mascot or military insignia; the scarf colour must not mimic a known airline livery.

### Budge (money and financial literacy)
- **Type / silhouette:** round badger with a white stripe down her head, a satchel across her body and a pencil tucked behind one ear.
- **Colour:** golden mustard `#E9A93B` body, cream stripe, brown satchel.
- **Personality:** practical, encouraging, a bit of a planner; loves lists and saving for big dreams (a telescope).
- **Voice direction:** friendly, clear, unhurried; never salesy.
- **State notes:** thinking = taps pencil on a notepad; celebrating = tosses notepad up and catches it; retry = scratches head and smiles.
- **Similarity check:** avoid piggy-bank tropes and coin-slot features (keeps her a character, not a bank); check badger characters in existing finance brands.

### Fenn (digital safety and cybersecurity)
- **Type / silhouette:** round fennec-fox monster with enormous ears (taller than her body), a magnifier monocle over one eye and a tiny detective satchel.
- **Colour:** indigo `#4F5BD5` body with lavender inner ears (distinct from Rumble's lighter blue by value and silhouette).
- **Personality:** sharp-eared, calm and kind; never scary or suspicious of the child; celebrates "telling a grown-up" as a detective move.
- **Voice direction:** quick, curious, reassuring; lowers voice slightly for "look closer" moments, never spooky.
- **State notes:** thinking = ears swivel; encouraging = raises magnifier; celebrating = ears spring up; retry = ears lower gently and perk back up.
- **Similarity check:** avoid trench coat + deerstalker combination (too close to a literary archetype used by many brands); avoid known fox mascots.

### Dabble (science)
- **Type / silhouette:** round newt-monster with a frilly head crest, a curly tail and oversized safety goggles.
- **Colour:** coral red `#FF6B6B` with a pale peach belly (distinct from Puff's pink by hue and from Bobbo's orange by redness).
- **Personality:** endlessly curious, a little messy, always asks "what if we test it?"; treats surprises as data.
- **Voice direction:** enthusiastic and clear; slows down for explanations.
- **State notes:** thinking = crest puffs up; celebrating = crest fans out with sparkles; retry = goggles fog, she wipes them and grins.
- **Similarity check:** axolotl-like designs are popular; keep the crest as a single frill and the body a short egg shape; avoid pink axolotl palettes.

### Pico (geography and world cultures)
- **Type / silhouette:** round hermit crab whose shell is a little globe covered with travel stickers; two big friendly eyes on short stalks; small claws.
- **Colour:** warm brown body `#A8714A`, globe shell in ocean blue and land green (not the exact Crew blue/green).
- **Personality:** adventurous homebody who loves pen pals and asking people about their lives; respectful and curious.
- **Voice direction:** friendly and wondering; pronounces place names carefully (recorded checks).
- **State notes:** greeting = waves a claw and spins shell; thinking = peeks out of shell; celebrating = shell spins with stickers flashing; retry = ducks in, pops out smiling.
- **Similarity check:** check existing hermit-crab characters; the globe shell is the signature.

### Snug (social and emotional skills)
- **Type / silhouette:** round wombat-like critter, low and wide, with large rounded ears and a small front pouch holding smooth "calm stones".
- **Colour:** soft peach `#FFBE98` with plum `#7A3E6B` nose and ear insides (distinct from Bobbo by lightness and silhouette).
- **Personality:** gentle, patient, a good listener; names feelings without judging; laughs easily.
- **Voice direction:** soft, warm, slow; never sing-song about sad feelings.
- **State notes:** her body colour **never changes with emotion** (to avoid colour-coded emotion tropes); feelings show through face and posture. Encouraging = offers a calm stone; celebrating = gentle bounce; retry = tilts head, kind smile.
- **Similarity check:** avoid colour-changing emotion monsters and the look of any known feelings-themed characters.

### Brix (engineering and inventions)
- **Type / silhouette:** round beaver-monster with a safety-yellow hard hat and a flat tail marked like a ruler; two big front teeth.
- **Colour:** steel navy `#34557A` body, hat in sparkle yellow `#FFD43B`, tan tail.
- **Personality:** builder who loves testing things until they break, then fixing them better; proud of improvements, not perfection.
- **Voice direction:** hearty, practical, encouraging; enjoys a good "let's test it!"
- **State notes:** thinking = measures with tail; celebrating = hard hat pops up; retry = taps the weak part and nods.
- **Similarity check:** name and look checked against existing builder/beaver mascots and toy brands; avoid tool-brand colours together (yellow + black).

### Ripple (nature, oceans and environment)
- **Type / silhouette:** round sea-otter-monster floating on her back, with a kelp-leaf hat and a favourite shell held to her chest.
- **Colour:** seafoam `#8EE3C8` (lighter and greener than Gloop's teal; confirm side by side) with cream face.
- **Personality:** caring, observant, hopeful; loves noticing tiny things; turns worry into action.
- **Voice direction:** calm and bright, like a guide at an aquarium.
- **State notes:** idle = gentle bobbing (reduced motion: still); celebrating = spins in the water with bubbles; retry = floats, thinks, smiles.
- **Similarity check:** otters are common mascots; keep the kelp-leaf hat and shell as signatures and avoid real aquarium mascots.

## 4. Cameo helper rules
- A world has one **host** and one **helper**. The helper explains the concept at round start and after the first miss (GAMES_BRIEF), so the helper should be a character whose personality fits explaining (calm Rumble for feelings, counting Puff for repeats and coins).
- Cross-library cameos make the island feel connected (Zephy flies Pico's postcards; Budge keeps Brix's budget). A cameo character never teaches outside its own library's facts without the host present.
- Existing Crew members appear in other libraries only with their established personalities (Bobbo the eager worrier, Rumble the slow measurer, and so on).

## 5. Production estimate (for ROADMAP)
